Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about CCMS

How do I file a complaint?
Simply click "Lodge Complaint" and fill out the secure form with details about the alleged corruption. You can provide evidence files and remain completely anonymous throughout the process.
Is my complaint completely confidential?
Yes, our system uses end-to-end encryption and anonymous ID generation. Your personal information is never shared with the public and only authorized personnel can access complaint details.
How long does it take to resolve a complaint?
Resolution time varies based on complexity. We track Service Level Agreements (SLA) for different complaint types. You can monitor progress through your dashboard anytime.
Can I track my complaint status?
Absolutely! You can track your complaint status in real-time using your unique complaint ID. Updates are sent via email and are available on your personal dashboard.
What if I need to add more evidence?
You can upload additional evidence at any time before the investigation concludes. Contact support with your complaint ID and we'll assist you in attaching the files securely.
Is there whistleblower protection?
Yes, we provide comprehensive whistleblower protection including legal safeguards, identity protection, and confidential communication channels to ensure your safety.
